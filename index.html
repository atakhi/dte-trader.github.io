GEX learnings — field observations
How negative GEX actually behaves
Amplification ≠ explosive. Negative GEX amplifies moves but most negative GEX days are slow grinds in one direction — not waterfalls. Explosive days need a catalyst. Adjust expectation accordingly.
Negative GEX needs a trigger. The regime removes the pinning force but does not create the move. Institutional order flow or a macro event is still needed to start the trend. GEX sets the environment only.
Magnitude matters. A mildly negative reading (-$50M) produces mild trending. A deeply negative reading (-$2B+) with a catalyst produces the sharp moves. Always note the actual net GEX number not just the sign.
Large pre-market gap on negative GEX. If SPX gaps down 25pts+ overnight, amplification may be mostly done before your entry window opens. The trending move happened pre-market. Wait for a confirmed fresh leg — not continuation of overnight move.
Wall behavior — the critical distinction
Positive GEX walls bounce price. MMs are long gamma — they buy dips and sell rips mechanically. Call wall = real ceiling. Put wall = real floor. Pinning is not coincidence, it is MM hedging.
Negative GEX walls accelerate through. MMs are short gamma — they sell into dips and buy into rips, amplifying the move. Put walls on negative GEX days are acceleration zones not bounce zones. Do not expect a bounce at the wall.
Exhaustion bounces still happen. Even on negative GEX, a hard sell into a put wall can bounce if selling is exhausted — volume dries up + reversal candle = real buyers stepping in. These bounces are weaker and shorter than positive GEX wall bounces. Do not rely on them.
Wall crossed = role reversal. When price breaks below a put wall, that wall becomes resistance above — not support. On negative GEX, MMs sell into any bounce back toward it. When price breaks above a call wall, that wall becomes support below.
Wall position vs current price — what it means
Put wall above price (price broke below it). Bearish signal. Put wall is now resistance. On negative GEX: MMs sell into every bounce toward it. Next put wall below is the acceleration target. Do not expect recovery to the broken wall easily.
Call wall below price (price broke above it). Bullish signal. Call wall is now support. On negative GEX: MMs buy into every dip toward it. Next call wall above is the acceleration target.
Price between two put walls. Strong downside structure. Both put bars amplify selling on negative GEX. Highest conviction short setup — slow grind down days come from this structure.
Call wall and put wall within 5pts of each other. Contested zone. The open price determines directional bias — above both = bullish, below both = bearish. No middle ground. High conviction move once side is determined.
Options skew vs GEX — reading conflicts
GEX = structural positioning. Based on existing open interest. Tells you what MMs must do mechanically based on current positions. Changes slowly.
Options skew = fresh flow. Based on what traders are buying right now. Can shift overnight. Tells you where active money is positioning for tomorrow — but not why.
When GEX and skew conflict. Raise signal quality threshold. Do not pre-commit to either direction. Let the 15min chart at 10:15am be the final judge. Both signals pointing the same way = highest conviction.
Heavy call skew alone does not confirm bullish direction. Three reasons call premium gets expensive: genuine bullish buying, institutions hedging short positions, or traders positioning for a dead cat bounce. You cannot tell which from price alone.
When skew resolves to match GEX. If skew initially conflicts with GEX but then shifts to align with it during the session or overnight — that is the conflict resolving. Higher conviction for the direction both now agree on.
Entry timing — hard lessons
First EMA9 touch only. By the 2nd or 3rd touch, trend momentum is fading. The bounce is weaker each time. Your checklist cannot distinguish touch 1 from touch 3 visually — you must count manually. 2nd or 3rd touch = exhausted = do not enter.
Extended from VWAP = move is mature. If price is 25+pts from VWAP when you see the EMA9 setup, the institutional move that created the trend is already largely done. You are entering at exhaustion not continuation. Skip it.
GEX confirms environment — price action confirms entry. Negative GEX alone is not a signal. It sets conditions. The 15min VWAP slope + EMA9 alignment IS your order flow proxy — when institutional money commits to a direction it shows up there first.
Crowded setups get squeezed first. When GEX and skew both point bearish, every retail trader is short at the open. The open often produces a brief counter-rally to shake out weak shorts before the real move. Wait for 10:15am — do not enter at 10:00am on a perfectly aligned pre-market read.
No setup = the strategy working. Negative GEX days that produce explosive amplification are roughly 1 in 4 or 5. Most are slow grinds or muted trends. Waiting 3–4 days without a clean signal is not failure — it is selectivity, which is the edge.
